About

ABOUT THE COMPANY

In4rtech Solutions Ltd is an IT consultancy based in Manchester, UK. We help organisations understand their technology landscape, define the architecture they need, and put AI and automation to work in ways that are practical, governed and aligned to business goals.

We work with technology and business leaders who need a structured, objective view of their estate, whether that’s a focused Enterprise Architecture review or support across a longer transformation.

OUR WORK

How our work typically takes shape across Enterprise Architecture, AI and Automation.

  • Enterprise Architecture · typical engagement

    Landscape review & target architecture

    Challenge
    A complex, multi-platform estate with no shared view of what exists or where it should go.
    Approach
    Discover and assess the current landscape with business and technology stakeholders.
    Architecture
    Target-state architecture, rationalisation options and a sequenced roadmap.
    Intended outcome
    A shared plan that guides technology decisions and investment.
  • AI · typical engagement

    AI readiness & adoption

    Challenge
    Pressure to adopt AI without clarity on where it will genuinely help.
    Approach
    Assess data, processes and governance, then identify and prioritise use cases.
    AI
    AI strategy and roadmap, use-case design and integration with enterprise systems.
    Intended outcome
    AI adopted where it adds value, safely and under control.

    Challenge

    Approach
