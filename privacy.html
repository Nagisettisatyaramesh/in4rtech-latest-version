2. What we collect

Information you give us through the contact form

  • Your name and email address (required).
  • Your organisation (optional).
  • Your area of interest, subject and message.

Please do not include special category data (for example, health information) in your message.

Information you give us through the chat assistant

  • The questions you type into the chat assistant and the answers it gives.

Please do not share personal or confidential information in the chat. If you would like us to contact you, please use the contact form.

Information collected automatically

  • Technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested. Our hosting provider processes this to deliver the website securely and keep short-term server logs.
  • Analytics data about how you use the site, only if you consent to analytics cookies (see section 6).

9. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct inaccurate data;
  • ask us to delete your data;
  • ask us to restrict how we use your data;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests;
  • data portability, where applicable;
  • withdraw consent at any time (for example, to analytics cookies), without affecting processing before you withdrew it.

To exercise any of these rights, please use the contact form. We will respond within one month, and there is normally no charge.

10. Complaints

If you have concerns about how we use your data, please contact us first so we can try to resolve them. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO), the UK supervisory authority: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, or call 0303 123 1113.
